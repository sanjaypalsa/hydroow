Camozzi Pneumatic Products

Genuine Camozzi Automation Components — Complete Range, In Stock, Worldwide When a component fails on an automated line, the cost isn't just the part — it's every minute the line stands still. That's the reality that makes Camozzi pneumatic products worth specifying. Engineered in Italy and trusted across 70 countries, Camozzi delivers the consistency and durability that industrial automation demands. We supply the complete range of genuine Camozzi pneumatic components — cylinders, valves, fittings, sensors, and air treatment — all sourced through authorised channels. From the Camozzi pneumatic valve controlling your actuator circuit to the Camozzi air cylinder delivering linear force on the line, every part is built to perform and backed by manufacturer support.These are the automation components from Camozzi that engineers keep coming back to.

Camozzi Pneumatic Cylinders

Our cylinder range covers every application profile. The Camozzi pneumatic cylinder Series 61 is the benchmark for general purpose linear actuation to ISO 15552 - robust, interchangeable, with bore sizes 32mm to 100mm. Want a smaller footprint? The compact and mini series deliver. Stainless steel variants handle food, pharma, and washdown environments. Rodless cylinders solve long-stroke, low-footprint challenges. Through-rod models provide equal force in both directions. Whatever your installation demands, there's a Camozzi cylinder built for it.

  • ISO / ISO 15552 Cylinders : Standards-compliant, cross-brand interchangeable, wide bore range
  • Mini & Compact Cylinders : Space-constrained applications with no compromise on reliability
  • Stainless Steel Cylinders : Corrosion-resistant for food, pharma, and marine environments
  • Rodless & Through-Rod : Long-stroke and balanced-force application solutions

Camozzi Solenoid Valve & Directional Valves

The Camozzi solenoid valve range covers 3/2 and 5/2 directional switching in monostable and bistable configurations — fast response, long coil life, and standardised DIN connectors for easy maintenance. Key operated valves deliver physical lockout for LOTO compliance. Palm and manual valves handle operator interface stations. If your circuit needs it, Camozzi makes it.

Camozzi Pneumatic Fittings & Push-In Connectors

Reliable connections throughout. The Camozzi pneumatic fittings range — push-in connectors, tee fittings, and straight connectors — provides tool-free installation, positive tube retention, and clean-release removal. Specify Camozzi fittings with Camozzi cylinders and valves for a matched, single-quality-standard circuit from end to end.

Sensors, Filter Regulators & Accessories

Camozzi sensors - reed and proximity types - mount directly on the Camozzi cylinder bodies for clean, reliable end of stroke detection without external brackets. The Camozzi filter regulator FRL range ensures the supply of clean and stable air to each downstream component – the particulate filtration, pressure regulation and optional lubrication in a single modular assembly. Speed controllers give precise needle-valve control over cylinder speed. Silencers reduce exhaust noise across all standard port sizes. The Camozzi hydraulic shock absorber range absorbs end-of-stroke kinetic energy — protecting tooling, mounts, and the cylinder itself from impact fatigue over thousands of cycles.
