Aventics Pneumatic Components & Automation Systems

Aventics (now part of Emerson) is synonymous with smart, reliable pneumatic technology. Born from the legacy of Bosch Rexroth pneumatics, Aventics components are engineered to deliver precise control, long lifecycles, and seamless integration into modern IIoT (Industrial Internet of Things) environments. Whether you are building a new automated packaging line or replacing a failing cylinder on a legacy machine, Aventics delivers where it counts: efficiency and uptime.

At Hydroow, we supply a comprehensive range of genuine Aventics pneumatic components, including ISO cylinders, valve islands, air preparation units, and sensors. If you need a fast replacement to stop an air leak and get a line running, or you are looking for advanced IO-Link enabled valve terminals, you will find the right fit here.

Aventics components stand out because they bridge the gap between traditional compressed air power and modern digital automation. Their valve islands are renowned for being lightweight, highly modular, and easy to network via fieldbus and industrial Ethernet. Their pneumatic cylinders meet strict ISO standards while offering advanced cushioning technology that reduces wear and vibration.
