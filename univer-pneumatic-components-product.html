Univer Pneumatic Components & Clamping Systems

Since 1971, Univer has been a pioneering force in Italian industrial pneumatics, recognized globally for producing rugged, highly reliable components that form the backbone of modern automation. From standard ISO cylinders powering general manufacturing lines to their world-renowned heavy-duty power clamps used in tier-one automotive assembly and welding, Univer delivers consistency and strength.

At Hydroow, we supply a comprehensive catalog of genuine Univer pneumatic components. Whether you need a KE-series ISO 15552 cylinder to restore a packaging machine or a specialized swing clamp cylinder to eliminate downtime on a robotic welding cell, we have the stock and the technical expertise to get your system back online.

Univer components are designed with a heavy focus on high-cycle durability and ease of integration. Their ISO cylinders (like the KE and KD series) are built to strict global standards, featuring wear-resistant seals and advanced cushioning for long service life. Furthermore, their automotive clamping division sets the industry standard for toggle lever power clamps and pin clamps, engineered specifically to withstand the harsh environments of automotive body-in-white welding and heavy material handling.
