Pneumax Pneumatic Components & Automation Systems

Pneumax stands as a leader in European pneumatic automation, known for delivering high-performance components that balance robust Italian engineering with modern digital integration. Whether you are building a new automated packaging line or replacing a failing cylinder on a legacy manufacturing machine, Pneumax delivers efficiency, repeatability, and reliable air control.

At Hydroow, we supply a comprehensive range of genuine Pneumax pneumatic components, including ISO cylinders, solenoid and spool valves, FRL air preparation units, and vacuum technology. If you need a fast replacement to stop an air leak and get your production line running again, you will find the exact match right here.

Pneumax components are built to last in demanding industrial environments. Their pneumatic cylinders, including the popular ECOPLUS series, meet strict ISO 15552 standards while offering advanced cushioning technology that minimizes wear and vibration. Their Optyma valve islands provide highly modular, networked control for complex systems, easily communicating via industrial fieldbus. By choosing Pneumax, you ensure your compressed air system operates smoothly, safely, and with minimal maintenance.
