Bürkert Process Valves & Fluid Control Instrumentation

Bürkert has spent decades building a reputation in precision fluid control: - not through marketing, but through valves and instrumentation that keep dosing, measuring, and controlling accurately in demanding process environments. Their solenoid valves, process valves, and sensors show up in water treatment, food and beverage, pharmaceutical, and industrial process lines worldwide.

At Hydroow, we supply genuine Bürkert components: - solenoid and process valves, pneumatic actuators, flow meters, and process instrumentation. Whether you’re specifying a new fluid control skid or replacing a valve that just failed on a running line, you’ll find the right part here, correctly matched to your system.

Bürkert parts are built to integrate into modern process automation and fluid handling systems without a fight. When precision and repeatability matter, these are the components worth sourcing right the first time.
