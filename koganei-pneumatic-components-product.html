Koganei Pneumatic Components & Automation Systems

Koganei has built its reputation around precision automation components that help manufacturers control motion, air, vacuum and specialized processes in compact packages. Its current product portfolio spans pneumatic cylinders and air valves, vacuum equipment, filter regulators, speed controllers, sensors, electric actuators, ionizers, fluid-control equipment and products for clean and semiconductor environments. Koganei's official materials also highlight its long history, with the company established in 1934 and pneumatic equipment manufacturing beginning in 1964.

At Hydroow, the Koganei range can be presented as a practical sourcing hub rather than a simple parts list. The supplied catalogue contains 200 Koganei entries covering cylinders, valves, air preparation, vacuum, handling, robot accessories, static elimination, pumps, sensors, clean systems, fluid control and specialized semiconductor products. Whether a customer is replacing a cylinder on an automated machine or sourcing a compact vacuum component, the page should help them reach the right family and model quickly.

The catalogue includes round body, pen, swing, twist, compact, basic, mini, block, guided, vacuum, rodless, slide, twin-rod and other air-cylinder formats, alongside grippers and pneumatic hands. Representative models include DA, PBDA, SDA, TWDA, SGDA, MBDA, KDV, ORGA, JSG, TDA and GR-series products. These components form the core of pneumatic motion and handling systems where reliable linear or rotary movement is required.
