Norgren Pneumatic Components Distributor

We are an approved distributor for IMI Norgren and supply genuine pneumatic components, with manufacturer warranties and specialist technical support. Norgren’s industry-leading motion and fluid control technology serves the manufacturing, automotive, packaging, food processing, life sciences, and industrial automation industries. All our products are genuine, traceable, and made to the highest quality standards.

Valves & Air Control Solutions

Our valve inventory includes solenoid valves, directional control valves, manifold systems, proportional valves, and specialty fluid control products. Industry 4.0 protocols for smart manufacturing environments are incorporated in many Norgren valves, which are engineered for fast response and long service life. Proportional valves offer precise electrical-to-pneumatic conversion for applications such as tension control, force regulation and variable speed systems.

Cylinders & Air Preparation

We stock a wide range of compact, ISO, NFPA, roundline, rodless and stainless steel cylinders to provide accurate linear motion for tough applications. Our air preparation range includes modular Norgren Excelon Plus FRL systems that provide clean and regulated compressed air to extend equipment life and reduce maintenance costs. Our product range is complemented by pressure relief and soft-start valves to protect the system.
