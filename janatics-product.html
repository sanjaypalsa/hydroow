Janatics Pneumatic Products — Authorized Dealer & Distributor

India’s industrial sector thrives on precision and Janatics has spent decades earning its place as one of the most reliable pneumatic brands in the country. We are an authorized Janatics dealer and distributor offering the full range of Janatics pneumatic products from high-cycle actuators to complete air treatment systems with verified authenticity and hands-on application support that generic online marketplaces simply cannot match.

Janatics Pneumatic Solutions for Industrial Automation

All Janatics components are designed with the reality of manufacturing in India in mind – varying air quality, high ambient temperatures, demanding shift cycles and the need for low maintenance reliability. Janatics pneumatic components are easy to install and are available off-the-shelf. They offer long operational life with easy maintenance in automotive assembly, textile manufacturing, pharmaceutical processing, food packaging and general-purpose factory automation, unlike imported alternatives that need frequent recalibration or specialist servicing.

Complete Range of Janatics Pneumatic Components

The range of cylinders includes standard double-acting models, compact bore types, guided cylinders and magnetic rodless models. Stroke force and positioning accuracy are consistent from model to model which is critical for high speed assembly and clamping applications where repeatability impacts the quality of the output.
