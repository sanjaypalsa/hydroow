ASCO Solenoid Valves & Pneumatic Controls

ASCO has been the reference name in solenoid valve technology for decades, not because of a marketing push, but because its valves keep controlling flow reliably long after cheaper alternatives fail. From the original Red Hat design to today’s NAMUR-mount and digital control valves, ASCO components show up in water treatment plants, process industries, dust collection systems, and pneumatic automation lines worldwide.

At Hydroow, we supply genuine ASCO valves: general service solenoid valves, NAMUR pilot valves, manifolds, and air preparation equipment. Whether you’re specifying a new pneumatic circuit or replacing a valve that just failed on a running line, you’ll find the right part here, correctly matched to your system.

ASCO valves are built to drop into modern and legacy fluid control systems without a fight. When tight shut-off and long service life matter, these are the components worth sourcing right the first time.
