Emerson Automation & Instrumentation Parts

Emerson has built its name on components that keep process plants running. Not through marketing, but through decades of reliable measurement, control, and automation hardware. Rosemount transmitters, Fisher control valves, ASCO solenoid valves, and DeltaV control systems show up across oil & gas, power generation, chemical processing, and manufacturing plants worldwide because they hold up under continuous duty.

At Hydroow, we supply genuine Emerson components across measurement instrumentation, control valves, pneumatics, automation platforms, and asset monitoring. Whether you’re specifying a new instrumentation loop or replacing a transmitter that just failed mid-shift, you’ll find the part here, correctly matched to your system.

Emerson parts are built to integrate into modern and legacy process control systems alike. When plant uptime and measurement accuracy matter, these are the components worth sourcing right the first time.
