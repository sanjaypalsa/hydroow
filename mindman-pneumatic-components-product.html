Mindman Pneumatic Components & Automation Systems

Mindman has built its business around pneumatic and automation components used in industrial equipment, with a product range spanning directional control valves, air treatment units, pneumatic cylinders, vacuum components, sensors, fittings, and end-of-arm tooling. Mindman Industrial was established in 1979 and is headquartered in Taiwan, with manufacturing and engineering operations supporting a broad international sales network.

At Hydroow, the supplied Mindman catalogue covers 200 product entries across 39 product types, giving maintenance teams and automation engineers a practical starting point for sourcing cylinders, valves, FRL units, pressure devices, vacuum components, grippers, fittings, tubing, and related accessories.
