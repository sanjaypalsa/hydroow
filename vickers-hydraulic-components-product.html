Vickers Hydraulic Components & Automation Products

Vickers has been a name hydraulic engineers trust for decades — long before it carried anyone else's logo. Today the brand sits under Danfoss Power Solutions, after a run inside Eaton that started in 1999. The pumps, valves, and motors haven't changed character. What you're buying is still the same rugged, field-proven hardware that's been running presses, winches, and mobile equipment since the vane pump made its name.

Hydroow stocks 200 genuine Vickers part numbers across 62 series — pumps, directional and proportional valves, pressure and relief valves, check and cartridge valves, motors, flow control valves, and the electronics that drive them. Whether your equipment was built last year or twenty years ago, you can get the part it actually needs.

Vickers Inc. joined Eaton in 1999 as part of the Aeroquip-Vickers acquisition, and the Vickers name carried on as Eaton's hydraulics brand for over two decades. In August 2021, Eaton sold its entire hydraulics division — Vickers included — to Danfoss Power Solutions. Nothing about the engineering changed hands separately; the same design lineage and part numbering carried over. If your equipment nameplate says "Eaton Vickers," the modern replacement is a current Vickers by Danfoss part — not a different product line.
